Heat treatment for blades and engineering components.

Heat treatment changes how a metal performs by heating and cooling it in a controlled way. It decides how a blade or component holds up in use.

We heat-treat steel, aluminium and titanium, for blades and for engineering components such as bolts, nuts and shafts. Each job is reviewed on its own: the material, the size and what the part will be used for, before we confirm we can take it on.

Ask about a job

What to send us

  • The material, if you know it
  • The dimensions of the part
  • What it will be used for

We’ll review the details and tell you whether we can take the job.

What we can’t take

Large industrial parts and anything beyond the capacity of the workshop.
