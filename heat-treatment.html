Workshop service

Heat treatment for blades and smaller components.

Heat treatment is a controlled process that changes the properties of steel through carefully managed heating and cooling. It plays an essential role in how a finished blade or component performs.

At koφki, I offer heat treatment for blades, tools and smaller metal or engineering components. Each job is considered individually, with the material, dimensions and intended application taken into account before confirming whether it is suitable for the service.

The service

Considered case by case.

The right heat treatment depends on the steel and what the finished part needs to do. Before accepting a job, I’ll discuss the material, the component and its intended use so that the requirements are clear.

The service is intended for blades, tools and smaller engineering components. Large industrial parts and components outside the capacity of the workshop are not suitable.

If you have a blade or a smaller steel component that needs heat treatment, get in touch with its material and intended application. I can review the details and let you know whether I can take it on.
