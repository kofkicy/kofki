Ask about a job

Heat treatment for blades and small steel parts.

Heat treatment changes how steel performs by heating and cooling it in a controlled way. It decides how a blade or component holds up in use.

I heat-treat blades, tools and smaller metal or engineering components. Each job is reviewed on its own: the material, the size and what the part will be used for, before I confirm I can take it on.

Ask about a job

What to send me

  • The material, if you know it
  • The dimensions of the part
  • What it will be used for

I’ll review the details and tell you whether I can take the job.

What I can’t take

Large industrial parts and anything beyond the capacity of the workshop.
