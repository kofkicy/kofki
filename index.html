How a commission works

From sketch to sheath

  • 01

    You describe the knife

    What it's for, how it'll be used, and any steel or handle material you have in mind. No CAD file needed — a rough sketch or a few reference photos is plenty.

  • 02

    You get a quote and timeline

    Every piece is priced to its steel, handle material, and hours at the forge. Most commissions are quoted within a few days of a first conversation.

  • 03

    It's forged, ground, and finished by hand

    Typical turnaround is 4–10 weeks depending on the queue and complexity. You'll get photos at rough-grind and again before final finishing.

  • 04

    It ships sharp, with a fitted saya or sheath

    Every knife leaves the workshop in Cyprus hand-sharpened, with a fitted blade cover included. Shipping is standard across the EU, with UK, US, and AU available on request.
