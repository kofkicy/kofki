How a commission works

  1. Tell me what you need

    How you cook, carry or use your knife. A rough sketch or a few reference photos is plenty.

  2. We design it

    I recommend the geometry, steel, dimensions and handle, then send a quote and timeline, usually within a few days.

  3. I build it by hand

    Shaped, ground, heat-treated, finished and sharpened in my Cyprus workshop, with progress photos along the way.

  4. It ships to you

    Hand-sharpened, with a fitted saya or sheath. Standard shipping across the EU; UK, US and Australia on request.

Tell me what you need

You don’t need to know the steel or the profile. Describe the job and we’ll work out the details.
