How it works

How a custom order works

  1. Tell us what you need

    A rough sketch or a few reference photos is plenty. You can also just describe the blade, for example: “Chef gyuto, 220 mm, olive wood handle.”

  2. We design it

    We recommend the edge geometry, steel type, heat treatment and other details, then send a quote and timeline, usually within a few days.

  3. We build it by hand

    Shaped, ground, heat-treated, finished and sharpened in our workshop in Cyprus.

  4. It ships to you

    Hand-sharpened, with a fitted saya or sheath. Free shipping in Cyprus, standard shipping across the EU, and other countries on request.
